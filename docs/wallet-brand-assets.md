# Wallet brand assets

The sign-in chooser uses the official wallet marks so users can identify the connector they are selecting. The local copies keep the chooser available without a third-party image request.

| Wallet | Local asset | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| MetaMask | `public/wallets/metamask.svg` | [MetaMask brand assets](https://metamask.io/en-GB/assets), official logo pack (`MetaMask-icon-fox.svg`) |
| Phantom EVM | `public/wallets/phantom.svg` | [Phantom press kit](https://phantom.com/learn/blog/introducing-phantom-s-new-brand-identity), official press kit logomark (`Phantom-Icon-Purple.svg`) |

Both SVGs are served locally and displayed with `object-fit: contain`; the source artwork is unchanged.
